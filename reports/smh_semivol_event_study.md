# SMH SEMI-VOL: price-led volatility-expansion event study

Source: Yahoo Finance adjusted daily SMH closes. Sample: 2010-02-02 to 2026-08-14.

## Construction and signal

**SEMI-VOL** = 20-session annualized standard deviation of SMH log returns, in volatility points. Its MACD line is the conventional 12/26 EMA difference. An episode begins when that MACD crosses from ≤0.5 to >0.5, and ends only on a down-cross that remains below 0.5 for five sessions.

A **constructive** cross additionally requires: SEMI-VOL was in the lower 35% of its trailing 252-session distribution five sessions earlier, an SMH close above the prior 20-session high, and SMH > 50-day average > 200-day average. These are all known at the close; thresholds are descriptive and were not fitted to outcomes.

The table shows mean return / fraction positive. Small-N subgroup results are descriptive, not proof of a standalone trading rule.
